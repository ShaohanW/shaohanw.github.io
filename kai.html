Kitchen AI (KAI)

Our team built a Raspberry Pi 5 assistant that reads grocery receipts, tracks ingredients and answers spoken questions. I worked on the device firmware and e-paper screen.

From receipt to inventory

Holding a receipt in front of the USB camera starts the workflow. OCR extracts grocery items, and the application records them in a structured inventory that can be queried later. The same camera can read printed recipes.

During cooking, a gesture provides a hands-free control. The microphone picks up a question, the display shows the response, and the speaker reads it aloud.

Founding team

Jingtao Zhao — Hand gesture recognition.

The team and the result

Our four-person team brought together students from the University of Michigan, Ohio State University and the University of Chicago. KAI won Overall 1st Place at Eastern Michigan University’s inaugural Hack The Nest, which drew more than 100 project submissions.
